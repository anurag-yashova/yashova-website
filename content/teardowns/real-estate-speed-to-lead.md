## What we did

Clicked the ad. Submitted a genuine enquiry with a working number. Recorded what happened next and when. That is the whole methodology, and it is more revealing than most paid audits.

## The number that matters

Cost per lead on this campaign is almost certainly excellent. Property enquiries in Gurgaon can be bought at ₹30–₹60 all day with a broad creative and no qualification.

Cost per **site visit** is a different number entirely, and it is the only one that pays salaries.

A funnel producing 400 leads a month at ₹30 looks like a triumph on the dashboard. If eleven of them visit the property, the real cost per visit is around ₹1,090 — and that is before anyone considers how many visits convert.

## Where the eleven hours go

This is not laziness. It is almost always a systems gap.

Leads land in a spreadsheet or a CRM. Someone opens it in the morning. Calls begin after the daily meeting. By then the enquiry is twelve hours cold and the buyer has spoken to two competitors who happened to have someone on shift.

The fix is not "call faster." Telling a sales team to work harder does not change the structure. The fix is an automated first touch that lands within seconds, so the buyer knows they have been heard and the human call has something to follow up rather than initiate.

A message containing the project name, location, starting price and a floor plan link costs nothing per lead and buys you a place in a conversation that would otherwise happen without you.

## What price on request actually costs

Withholding price is a habit inherited from print advertising, where space was expensive and the phone call was the point.

Online it works against you twice. It attracts everyone regardless of budget, so you pay for clicks from people who could never buy. And it signals hesitancy — buyers read it as "expensive, and they know it."

Stating a starting price, or even an EMI, does the filtering before you pay for it.

## What we would change first

1. Automated WhatsApp acknowledgement within sixty seconds of submission.
2. Two qualification questions in the form: budget bracket and timeline.
3. Starting price in the ad and above the fold on the page.
4. A structured human follow-up sequence over fourteen days, not one call.
5. Location targeting restricted to people living in the area.
6. Redefine the conversion event as a booked site visit, fed back to the platform, so the algorithm starts optimising for people who show up rather than people who fill forms.

That last one is the difference between a lead generation campaign and a sales campaign.

## Caveat

We saw one enquiry, not their CRM. It is possible we caught them on a bad evening, and it is possible their close rate is fine. But the absence of any automated acknowledgement is structural, not circumstantial — that gap exists for every lead, every night.
